🏆 Prestige?

Open the 📖 guide beside hints to see your missing elements and their recipe steps.

Requires Shop and Star Forge unlocked. Collect 100 ranked elements for your first Prestige, then 25 more per completed Prestige (100, 125, 150…). These are fixed counts, independent of the total number of elements. Keys start at 25 and coins at 100,000; each Prestige adds 6.25 keys (rounded up) and 25,000 coins, capped at 250 keys and 1,000,000 coins. Any ranked elements count. Rank-excluded and exclusive elements are not required.

Confirming spends the displayed key requirement and resets normal elements, all normal coins, remaining non-exclusive keys, crafting history, recipe locks and ALL upgrade levels. Exclusive elements, CyMoney and prestige totals are kept. ALL upgrade levels reset, including Prestige Shop purchases. You receive 1 prestige coin, Shop, an unupgraded Star Forge, a Prestige counter element and one additional starting basic element.

🏆 Prestige Shop

Every level costs 1 prestige coin. Purchases survive ordinary Reset but all upgrade levels reset on Prestige. Forge upgrades apply on the next activation; stock upgrades apply on the next restock.

💎 CyMoney Workshop

Each CyMoney element produces 1 CyMoney per minute while this tab is active. Upgrade prices rise with each level. CyMoney upgrades reset on both Prestige and ordinary Reset.
